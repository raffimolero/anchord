All about Anchors. Moving anchors is everything.
use the whole keyboard to navigate every step of the way. 30 keys available, use them for alternating vertical/horizontal movement.
look at a point, press the "Jump" key, each line turns into a character. press that character to jump to that line.
then, the line reveals itself, and the lines above and below it show a horizontal sequence of characters that you can jump to.
3 key presses to jump to a 30 by 30 square centered on your current cursor.
